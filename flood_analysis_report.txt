Pakistan Flood Analysis Report
==================================================

1. Dataset Overview:
- Total flood events recorded: 19
- Time period covered: 1992.0 to 2022.0
- Locations affected: 16 distinct regions

2. Key Findings:

Temporal Patterns:
- 12 of 19 flood events (63.2%) occurred during monsoon months (June-September)

Most Deadly Flood Events:
- 1992 in Azad Kashmir,Punjab,Jammu and Kashmir,North-West Frontier Province: 2500 deaths
- 2010 in Almost all Pakistan(Punjab,Sindh,KPK,Balochistan): 2000 deaths
- 2013 in Almost all Pakistan: 1700 deaths

Most Frequently Affected Regions:
- Sindh(Karachi): 3 flood events
- Azad Kashmir,Punjab,Jammu and Kashmir,North-West Frontier Province: 1 flood events
- Sindh: 1 flood events

Most Common Causes:
- Heavy monsoon rains: 8 occurrences
- Heavy rainfall: 3 occurrences
- Heavy rain: 2 occurrences

3. Recommendations:
- Strengthen flood preparedness and early warning systems before monsoon season
- Focus infrastructure improvements in frequently affected regions
- Address root causes like heavy rainfall and poor drainage systems
- Improve data collection on property damages for better impact assessment
